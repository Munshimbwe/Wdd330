HealthPulse Enterprise Suite

WDD 330 Final Project Site Plan

1. Product Overview

HealthPulse Enterprise Suite is an offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed for community health workers and clinicians in low-connectivity settings. It provides local pediatric malnutrition screening (WHO MUAC and respiration algorithms), multi-organ risk questionnaires, and secure HL7 FHIR v4 telehealth export capabilities.

2. Target Audience

  • Community health workers and traveling nurses operating in remote or rural environments.
  • Humanitarian medical volunteers requiring instant, offline clinical decision support.
  • Public health coordinators needing standardized, anonymized epidemiological data syncs.
